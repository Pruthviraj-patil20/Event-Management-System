FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions regarding ticket booking, QR passes, and event hosting.

How do I receive and scan my event ticket? +
Immediately after completing reservation, a cryptographically signed QR pass is generated in your browser and sent to your registered email address. You can also print it or present the digital pass from your smartphone at the venue entrance.
What is the refund policy if an event is rescheduled or canceled? +
All paid events hosted on EventSphere include a 100% money-back guarantee. If an organizer cancels or reschedules, your payment is automatically refunded back to your original payment method within 3-5 business days.
How can I list and host my own event on EventSphere? +
Simply click on "Create Event" or choose "Host an Event" in the contact form above. Our curation team will review your event details, set up custom ticket tiers, and activate your listing within 24 hours.
Can I transfer my pass to a colleague or friend? +
Yes! In your booking confirmation email, you will find a 1-click attendee name update link that allows reassigning the QR pass up to 2 hours prior to the event door opening.
